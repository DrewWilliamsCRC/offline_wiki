# Privacy policy for Wiki Reader

*Last updated: 27 November 2026*

Wiki Reader, for Android, iPhone and iPad, is an offline reader for Wikipedia. It collects no
information about you.

- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.** The app has none of these, and contains no
  third-party code that does.
- **What you read stays on the device.** The list of articles you read recently, the articles you
  saved (on iPhone and iPad), how far you read each one, and the text size you chose are kept on
  the device only, and are deleted with the app. You can remove articles from either list.
- **The network is used only to download, when you ask.** On its Downloads screen the app fetches
  the list of what can be downloaded and the search indexes from this project's page on GitHub
  (github.com and the servers it uses to deliver files), and Wikipedia itself from Kiwix
  (download.kiwix.org and its mirrors). Like any web server, they see your device's IP address and
  which files it asks for; their own privacy policies cover that. Nothing else is sent, and no
  information about you is sent with those requests. Reading never uses the network.
- **Files.** On Android the app reads and writes only in the one folder you choose for it. On
  iPhone and iPad it uses its own folder, and a folder you choose if you point it at one.
- **Notifications** on Android show how a download is getting on. They are made on the device.
- **Children.** The app collects no information from anyone, children included.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/DrewWilliamsCRC/offline_wiki/issues>.
